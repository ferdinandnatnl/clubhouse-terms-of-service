# Clubhouse Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

**Last updated: April 6, 2026**

# Privacy Policy

### What We Collect
1. When you sign up through Google, Discord, or Facebook, we receive your name, email, and profile picture from that provider. We use this to set up your account.
2. We keep track of the clubs you join, bookmark, and events you're interested in to personalize your experience.
3. We store some preferences locally on your device (cached images) to make the app feel faster.

### How We Use Your Information
1. Your info helps us run your account smoothly and show you relevant content. For example, we'll suggest clubs based on your interests.
2. We'll send you important updates about your account or changes to our platform but we will not spam you.
3. Club organizers can see a list of members who joined their club, but only your name and profile picture.

### How We Protect Your Information
1. We will never sell your personal data to advertisers or other third parties.
2. Your information is stored on secure servers (hosted by Supabase), and only authorized personnel can access it.
3. Authentication is handled by your chosen provider (Google, Discord, or Facebook). We never see or store your password.

### Third-Party Services
We use the following services to run Clubhouse:
- **Supabase** — database and authentication
- **Google, Discord, Facebook** — sign-in providers (their own privacy policies apply)

We only share what's necessary with these services for Clubhouse to work. We don't share your data with anyone else.

## Terms of Service

### For Club Organizers
1. You're responsible for your content. If you organize a club, you're in charge of the content you post about your club and events.
2. We might remove content that goes against these terms or our community guidelines.
3. By posting, you allow us to show your content to other Clubhouse users and promote your club or events within the app.

### Disclaimers and Limitations
1. We offer the app as it is and can't promise it'll always be perfect or available.
2. We're not liable for the accuracy of club or event information posted by organizers.
3. We're always working to improve Clubhouse, so we might add, change, or remove features occasionally.

### Account Deletion
You can delete your account at any time by emailing us at tryclubhouse@gmail.com. Once we receive your request, we will permanently delete your account and all associated data within 30 days.

If you signed up with Facebook, you can also remove Clubhouse from your Facebook settings. See our [Data Deletion Instructions](https://clubhouse.so/data-deletion-instructions) for details.
