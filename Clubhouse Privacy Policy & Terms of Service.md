# Clubhouse Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

**Last updated: April 6, 2026**

## Privacy Policy

Clubhouse is built to help people discover clubs and events more easily. This Privacy Policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and when information is visible to others.

### Information We Collect

#### **1. Account Information**
When you sign up using Google, Discord, or Facebook, we receive basic account information from that provider, such as your **name**, **email address**, and **profile picture**. We use this to create and manage your Clubhouse account.

#### **2. Activity on Clubhouse**
We collect information related to how you use Clubhouse, including:
- clubs you join
- clubs you bookmark
- events you are interested in
- registrations you submit
- comments, images, and other content you post

We use this information to operate the platform, personalize your experience, and improve the service.

#### **3. Device and Local Data**
We may store limited data locally on your device, such as cached images and app preferences, so Clubhouse loads faster and feels smoother to use.

#### **4. Event Registration Data**
If you register for an event using a built-in Clubhouse form, we collect the information you submit through that form. That information may be shared with the relevant club organizer so they can manage the event.

### How We Use Information

We use your information to:
- create and manage your account
- show relevant clubs, events, and recommendations
- support club and event features
- communicate important account or platform updates
- maintain, improve, and secure Clubhouse
- prevent abuse, misuse, or unauthorized activity

We do **not** sell your personal data to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

### What Other People Can See

Some information is visible to others when you use Clubhouse.

- If you join a club, the club organizer may see your **name** and **profile picture**.
- If you comment on a club page, your **comment**, images, and profile information may be visible to other users.
- Club admins and platform admins may remove content that violates rules or guidelines.

### How We Share Information

We only share information when necessary to operate Clubhouse.

We may share limited data with:
- **Supabase** — backend and database infrastructure
- **Google, Discord, Facebook** — sign-in providers

These services have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices outside Clubhouse.

We do **not** share your data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.

### How We Protect Information

We take reasonable steps to protect your information.

- Data is stored on secure infrastructure used to operate Clubhouse.
- Access is limited to authorized personnel.
- Authentication is handled by your sign-in provider.
- We use safeguards to reduce the risk of unauthorized access or misuse.

No system is completely secure, but we aim to protect your data responsibly.

### Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to:
- provide the service
- comply with legal obligations
- resolve disputes
- enforce our terms

If you request deletion, your data will be removed within **30 days**, unless retention is required for legal or operational reasons.

### Account Deletion

You can request deletion of your account at any time by emailing **tryclubhouse@gmail.com**.

We will delete your account and associated data within **30 days**, subject to legal or operational requirements.

If you signed up with Facebook, you may also remove Clubhouse from your Facebook settings. See our **Data Deletion Instructions** for details.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are significant, we will update the **Last updated** date and may notify you through the app or email.

### Contact Us

If you have questions, contact us at **tryclubhouse@gmail.com**.
